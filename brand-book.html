Brand Book

A curated public reference for using the Dipnot brand consistently — logo, color, type, and usage rules.

Full internal brand specification (extended): Dipnot Brand Book by Gingersauce

Logo

The Dipnot mark comes in solo marks (transparent background, for in-layout use) and composed variants (mark on a solid background, for app icons, OG cards, and social avatars). Pick the variant that reads cleanly against its background.

Rules

Clear space

Leave at least 1/6 of the mark height empty on all sides of any solo mark. No text, button, or container edge may enter this zone. If the mark renders at 60 px, keep 10 px clear on each side. Clear space is a minimum, not a target — more is fine, less is not. Composed variants already bake in the equivalent padding; don't add more around them.

Minimum size

The mark holds up small, but inner detail muddies below 16 px. Floors per context:

Context Minimum
Favicon / browser tab 16 × 16 px
Inline UI (button icon, nav item, chip) 20 × 20 px
Standalone UI (avatar, card corner, list row) 40 × 40 px
Display (hero, splash, marketing) 80 × 80 px
Print 8 mm

Misuse

Don't:

  • Stretch or skew the mark.
  • Recolor outside the approved palette.
  • Add shadows, glows, or outlines.
  • Place the mark on low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Rotate or flip the mark.
  • Use the Winter Bloom or Sky Captain colors as UI backgrounds — they exist only for composed brand assets.
