About us

Who we are

Dipnot is an independent platform for anyone interested in diving and the underwater world. From diving news and safety to gear and technology, caves and wrecks, the environment and travel, we bring carefully written articles together in one app. The articles are in Turkish.

We started in Istanbul in 2025. Our aim is to be a simple, reliable place to follow what's happening in the diving world, for everyone from beginners to experienced divers.

Why “Dipnot”?

Dipnot is Turkish for footnote: a short but valuable note at the bottom of a page. It's also a play on words, since dip means the bottom of the sea. Everything learned underwater has a footnote, and we write them.
