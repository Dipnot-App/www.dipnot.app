# Change History

obligations or how your data is processed, and updates that are only
in your favour, newest first, with the version number. Changes to
wording, spelling or layout are not listed.

## Privacy Policy

- **28.10.2026 · Version 2.0** — The legal basis for account information is now establishing and performing the contract; for competition data it is now legitimate interest; the phone number, never collected, removed. One-account-per-inbox check, usage statistics, newsletter and contact form, website cookies and the security cookie on the account deletion pages added (Sections 2, 4). Service providers gathered in one table and the cross-border transfer statement corrected (Section 3). Reopening your account within 30 days and what is kept after deletion added: a deletion record kept 5 years, usage statistics kept 14 months (Section 5). How to make a request and complain explained; contact address is now <info@dipnot.app> (Sections 1, 6). Updates are now shown in the app and you are asked to state that you have read and understood them (Section 9).

## Terms of Use
