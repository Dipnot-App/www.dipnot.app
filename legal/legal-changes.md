# Change History

Updates to Dipnot's legal texts that change your rights, your
obligations or how your data is processed, and updates that are only
in your favour, newest first, with the version number. Changes to
wording, spelling or layout are not listed.

## Privacy Policy

- **25.11.2026 · Version 2.1** — Competition data now states that the times you cast or withdrew each vote are also processed, and that they are used to fix votes and apply the withdrawal window (Section 2).
- **29.10.2026 · Version 2.0** — The legal basis for account information is now establishing and performing the contract; for competition data it is now legitimate interest; the phone number, never collected, removed. One-account-per-inbox check, usage statistics, newsletter and contact form, website cookies and the security cookie on the account deletion pages added (Sections 2, 4). Service providers gathered in one table and the cross-border transfer statement corrected (Section 3). Reopening your account within 30 days and what is kept after deletion added: a deletion record kept 5 years, usage statistics kept 14 months (Section 5). How to make a request and complain explained; contact address is now <info@dipnot.app> (Sections 1, 6). Updates are now shown in the app and you are asked to state that you have read and understood them (Section 9).

<a id="terms-of-use"></a>

## Terms of Use

- **29.10.2026 · Version 2.0** — You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide (Section 3). Updates are now announced in the app and take effect with your acceptance; e-mail notice and "continued use means acceptance" removed (Section 10).

## Competition Rules

### 2026 Competition

- **25.11.2026 · Version 1.1** — From 1 December 2026 00:00 (TSİ), every vote cast so far is fixed; a vote cast after that can be withdrawn within 24 hours. This change takes effect on 1 December 2026 00:00 (TSİ) (Clauses 3, 4). Voting requires a verified e-mail address, one account per e-mail address and no disposable e-mail addresses, stated explicitly, and votes from accounts later found to use a disposable address are invalid; votes are counted per account (Clause 2). Recording the vote distribution at the close and setting the result after invalid votes are removed (Clause 6), and how deleting and reopening an account affects votes (Clause 3) explained. Votes of frozen accounts, organised mass actions, the right to be heard and the 15-day review period added (Clause 9). Why and for how long the candidate dive centres' details taken from the TSSF list are processed added; the vote records used to detect cheating and their retention explained (Clause 10).
- **29.10.2026 · Version 1.0** — Changes are now announced on the competition page in the app; Instagram announcements removed (Clause 11). The competition rules are published in Turkish only.
